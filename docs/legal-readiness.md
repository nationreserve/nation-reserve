# Legal Readiness

Legal document version and acceptance persistence is implemented in migration 0027. Acceptance records capture the exact document version, user, optional organization, timestamp, context and privacy-preserving request metadata hashes; records are append-only.

Publication remains blocked until counsel approves and publishes: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy where applicable, Acceptable Use Policy, Manufacturer Agreement, Hiring Company Agreement and Robot Owner Agreement. Repository pages are product drafts, not legal approval. Required open decisions include worker/owner classification, payments and chargebacks, taxes, warranties, robot/site safety, data processing, biometric/video implications, retention/deletion, international restrictions, dispute venue and electronic signatures.
