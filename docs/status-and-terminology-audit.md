# Status and Terminology Audit

Approved distinctions are enforced in review: scheduled versus verified operating time; accrued versus finalized versus invoiced; payment submitted/processing versus settled; financially ready versus ready for payout; payout submitted/processing versus paid. Scheduling alone is never chargeable, and frontend actions never establish processor settlement.

Potentially misleading marketplace, guaranteed earnings/uptime, paid scheduled hours, completed payment/payroll and available-balance language must be contextual correction or removed. Generated source scans are reviewed rather than blindly replacing legitimate policy text.
