# Prompt 017 handoff: Manufacturer Portal

The Manufacturer Portal must implement:

- A private opportunity inbox with recipient isolation and response deadlines.
- Expanded job responsibility, performance, environment, and safety review.
- Task-level questions and private clarification threads.
- Authorized training-package version access with expiry and revocation.
- Training-format and robot-system compatibility declarations.
- Site-assessment requests and outcomes.
- Direct private messaging and explicit contract-participant conversations.
- Contract negotiation, version comparison, decisions, and immutable approvals.
- Robot model proposals with capability and training compatibility evidence.
- Eligible robot allocation and manufacturer serial assignment.
- Production heartbeat credential and activation readiness.
- Inactive-report acknowledgement and replacement fulfillment.
- Fleet health, downtime, allocation, response, and fulfillment analytics.

Required isolation: a manufacturer cannot see another manufacturer's opportunity response, pricing, proposal, allocation, direct messages, or training access unless explicitly authorized in a shared contract context.
