# Prompt 023 Handoff

Prompt 023 must close GAP-022-001, 002, 005, 008 and 010 before launch; pilot blockers cover hardware connector certification, processor sandbox evidence, manual accessibility/responsive review and representative performance. Security review must test organization, manufacturer-response, location, training-grant, search, message and timeline isolation. Privacy review must cover training files, support access, exports and retention.

Legal assumptions requiring approval include contracts, owner classification, fees, refunds/chargebacks, data processing, retention, disclosures and manufacturer responsibilities. External requirements include payment-processor sandbox/production approval, cloud accounts and DNS/certificates, manufacturer credentials, supported hardware and accessibility/manual review personnel.

Recommended pilot restrictions: approved organizations and manufacturers only; one certified connector or manual-upload-only labeling; fake provider prohibited outside tests; capped fleet/location scope; read-only support by default; monitored payout batches; rollback and incident staffing; no claim of general availability.

No external approval or manual review is recorded by Prompt 022.
