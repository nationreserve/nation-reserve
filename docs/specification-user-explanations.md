# Specification User Explanations

Every major page must explain what the service means for its audience. Financial pages distinguish scheduled, verified, accrued, invoiced, submitted, settled, paid, held, financially ready, and payout-ready. Onboarding explains why information is required, who sees it, next steps, remaining activation conditions, failure, and recovery.

The validator flags misleading guarantees and status language. Explanation records identify required and prohibited messages and every page where the topic belongs.
