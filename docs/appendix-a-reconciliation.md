# Appendix A Reconciliation

Appendix A is the original authoritative product-rules baseline. It is preserved verbatim. Later approved specifications refine or supersede individual statements as follows.

| Appendix A area | Current interpretation | Controlling later source |
|---|---|---|
| Product mission “marketplace” wording | RoboWorkPool facilitates manufacturer fulfillment through open work orders and private opportunities; it does not automatically match parties. | Appendices M and N; approved contract-origin amendment |
| $5 verified operating rate | Remains 500 USD minor units per finalized verified operating hour. | Appendix J and Business Rule Constants |
| Platform fee described as one company-side 15% fee | Superseded by the approved current rule: 15% company fee plus 15% owner fee. One hour produces $5.75 company total, $4.25 owner net and $1.50 platform revenue. | Appendix J; approved financial amendment; Prompts 007 and 022 |
| “Owner receives full standardized operating payment” | Superseded by the owner-side 15% fee. Gross remains $5.00; net is $4.25 at current defaults. | Appendix J and approved financial amendment |
| Maximum 20 active robots | Current counting policy includes active, suspended, and maintenance states; retired/destroyed/decommissioned exclusions follow approved lifecycle rules. | Appendices L and M |
| Heartbeat as primary evidence | Refined to signed heartbeat evidence plus assignment, contract, schedule-window, state mapping, lifecycle eligibility and replay protection. Schedule alone produces zero verified time. | Appendices E0, E, L, M; Prompt 006 |
| Robot status examples | Implementations use orthogonal lifecycle, activation, operational, heartbeat, ownership, maintenance and financial-eligibility state dimensions rather than one overloaded status. | Appendix L |
| Downpayment Queue | Ordered by qualifying downpayment receipt; advances only after contract acceptance, fulfillment confirmation and purchase-order authorization. | Chapter 25 amendment and Business Rule Constants |

No original text is deleted. Engineers must use the current controlling source for the refined rule and retain Appendix A for historical provenance.
